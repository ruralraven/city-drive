# Zoom City

A 3D open-city driving game. Drive around, take jobs, collect coins, unlock cars, and pull off getaways.

Play here: https://ruralraven.github.io/city-drive/

This game is made for ages about 10 and up. There are no guns, no blood, and nobody gets hurt. People jump out of the way. Wanted stars go up if you crash into traffic, smash objects, or speed through a red light. Cartoon police cars will chase you. Hide to lose stars.

Jobs are deliveries, taxi rides, checkpoint races, and getaways.

## Controls

**On a phone**

- Left stick: walk, or steer when you are in a car
- Gas and Brake: on the right
- Nitro: a short speed burst
- Hop in: get in or out of a nearby car
- Bell: honk the horn

**On a keyboard**

- W A S D or arrow keys: walk, or drive (W / up is gas, S / down is brake)
- Shift: nitro
- E: hop in or out of a car
- Space: drift
- H: horn
- N: day and night
- Esc or P: pause

## Cars

Coins you collect are saved on this device. Use them in the Garage to unlock new cars. If a car gets too smashed, it pops in a cartoon explosion (with no one inside), and you can call another one from the Garage.

## Note

This game was tested in a desktop browser. It is built for iPhone Safari too, but it has not been tested on a real iPhone yet.
