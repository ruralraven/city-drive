# Zoom City

A 3D open-city driving game. Drive around, take jobs, collect coins, and pull off getaways. Every car is free.

Play here: https://ruralraven.github.io/city-drive/

This game is made for ages about 10 and up. There are no guns, no blood, and nobody gets hurt. People jump out of the way. Wanted stars go up if you crash into traffic, smash objects, or speed through a red light. Cartoon police cars will chase you. Hide to lose stars.

Jobs are deliveries, taxi rides, checkpoint races, and getaways.

## Controls

**On a phone**

- Left stick: walk, or steer when you are in a car
- Gas and Brake: on the right
- Nitro: a short speed burst
- Drift: hold to slide
- Hop in: get in or out of a nearby car
- Bell: honk the horn

**On a keyboard**

- W A S D or arrow keys: walk, or drive (W / up is gas, S / down is brake)
- Shift: nitro
- E: hop in or out of a car
- Space: drift
- H: horn
- N: day and night
- Esc or P: pause

## Cars

Every car is free from the start. Coins you collect are saved on this device and count as a score. If a car gets too smashed, it pops in a cartoon explosion (with no one inside), and you can call another one from the Garage.

If the police catch you, you will see a Busted! message and go back to the plaza. Your wanted stars are cleared. Nobody gets hurt.

## Play on an iPhone

Open the link in Safari. The game works in portrait and landscape. Turning the phone sideways gives you a bigger view.

To play it like an app:

1. Open [Zoom City](https://ruralraven.github.io/city-drive/) in Safari (not in another app's browser).
2. Tap the Share button (the square with the arrow pointing up).
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**, then Add.
4. Open the Zoom City icon on your Home Screen.

## Note

This game was tested in a desktop browser and in iPhone-sized browser tests. It is built for iPhone Safari, but it has not been tested on a real iPhone yet.
