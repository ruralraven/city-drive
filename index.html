Drive, take jobs, and pull off wild getaways.

How to play

  • Walk with the left stick, or the W A S D keys.
  • Hop into a car when you are next to it. On a keyboard, press E.
  • Gas and Brake are on the right. On a keyboard, use W and S, or the arrow keys.
  • Steer with the stick or A and D.
  • Go to the glowing job signs for deliveries, taxi rides, races, and getaways.
  • Five police cars patrol the city. They only chase if you have wanted stars.
  • Crashes, bumping a person, smashed stuff, or running a red light only raise wanted stars if a police car sees it. If nobody saw it, you get away with it. A bank alarm always calls the police.
  • There are a few banks. Drive into the glowing marker and stay a few seconds to grab cartoon money bags. The bank closes, then opens again later.
  • Try the stunt park, the gas station, the car wash, Zoom Burger, hidden stars, and the river jump.
  • Hold Nitro (or Shift) for a speed burst.
  • Every car is free. Collect coins just for fun and a high score.
  • People can get knocked over in a cartoon way, then pop back up. Nobody gets hurt. There are no guns.

On iPhone: tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen, to play it like an app.
