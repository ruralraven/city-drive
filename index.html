How to play

  • Walk with the left stick, or the W A S D keys.
  • Hop into a car when you are next to it. On a keyboard, press E.
  • Gas and Brake are on the right. On a keyboard, use W and S, or the arrow keys.
  • Steer with the stick or A and D.
  • Go to the glowing job signs for deliveries, taxi rides, races, and getaways.
  • Crashes, smashed stuff, and running red lights can raise wanted stars. Police cars will chase you. Hide to lose stars.
  • Hold Nitro (or Shift) for a speed burst.
  • Every car is free. Collect coins just for fun and a high score.
  • People jump out of the way. Nobody gets hurt. There are no guns.
